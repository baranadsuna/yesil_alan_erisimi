about=Calculates true street-network walking distances from residential blocks and buildings (connecting from the facade closest to the road leading to the target facility) to any urban facility (schools, green spaces, health centers, stations). Produces colored block/building polygons, mid-street clipped service area lines, non-overlapping walkability isochrones, Euclidean benchmark circles, pedshed ratios, and p-median demand allocation. Features automatic planar road noding, T-junction gap healing, low-threshold dock/float controls, and 5-language support.
