about=Accessibility Analysis is an all-in-one urban network accessibility and walkability toolkit for urban planners, GIS analysts, transport engineers, and researchers. Unlike simple circular buffers or centroid-only tools, it computes true street-network shortest paths (multi-source Dijkstra) with exact mid-edge splitting and connects residential blocks and buildings from their closest road-facing facade leading toward the target facility (schools, green spaces, hospitals, transit stations, emergency services). It automatically nodes intersecting streets, heals digitizing gaps and T-junction undershoots, and generates: (1) Prominent Blue Target Facility markers with capacity & demand statistics, (2) Colored Block/Building Accessibility Polygons and Points, (3) Clipped Service Area Street Networks at custom distance thresholds (e.g., 250m, 500m, 1000m), (4) Non-overlapping Walkability Isochrone Catchments, (5) Euclidean Benchmark Circles & Pedshed Walkability Ratios, and (6) Interactive HTML/Text Audit Reports with verified academic DOIs. Built-in runtime language switcher supports English, Turkish, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, and Indonesian. Full QGIS 3 & QGIS 4 (Qt5 & Qt6) compatibility.
